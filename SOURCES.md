## Confirmed as written

- **Emergency numbers:** 112, 100, 101, 108, 1091/181 ([Kerala LSG Dept](https://thalappalampanchayat.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/node/138)).
  The tourist helpline is 1363 / 1800-11-1363 ([Embassy of India](https://indembassysweden.gov.in/page/tourist-help-line-in-india)).
  Kerala Tourism's line is 1800-425-4747, from Indian numbers only ([Kerala Tourism footer](https://www.keralatourism.org/experiences/)).
- **Mattancherry Palace:** closed Fridays ([Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/destination/mattancherry-palace-kochi/178)).
- **Eravikulam:** closed Feb–Mar for calving, reopens 1 April, tickets booked online
  ([Outlook Traveller](https://www.outlooktraveller.com/News/keralas-eravikulam-national-park-reopens-after-breeding-season-closure)).
- **Periyar:** first boat at 7:30 am ([Abad](https://www.abadhotels.com/green-forest-resort-thekkady/blogs/periyar-boating.html), guide).
  Nature walk, bamboo rafting and their fees are on [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/ecotourism/trekking-programs/periyar-programme/30).
- **Meesapulimala:** KFDC permits only ([Onmanorama](https://onmanorama.com/travel/kerala/2018/01/15/meesappulimala-entry-pass-forest-department.html)).
- **Water Metro:** ₹20–40, about 7 am–8 pm, High Court–Fort Kochi route ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kochi_Water_Metro)).
- **Kochi–Munnar:** about 130 km, 4–5 h ([Fragrant Nature](https://www.fragrantnature.com/blogs/kochi-to-munnar-travel-guide-how-to-reach-best-route-distance-options.html), guide).
  Cab fare is about ₹3,300 ([Uber Intercity](https://www.uber.com/in/en/r/intercity/kochi-kerala-to-munnar-kerala/)).
- **Thamarassery ghat:** nine hairpin bends ([Kerala Tourism](https://keralatourism.org/destination/wayanad-ghats/271)).
  Kozhikode–Kalpetta is about 85 km ([StayVista](https://www.stayvista.com/blog/calicut-to-wayanad-85-km-9-hairpins-how-to-reach-2026-guide/), guide).
- **Temples:** Padmanabhaswamy dress code ([Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/faq/is-there-any-dress-code-for-padmanabha-swamy-temple)).
  Guruvayur is closed to non-Hindus ([The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/amp/story/kerala/allowing-non-hindus-temple-feast-will-affect-sanctity-guruvayur-chief-priest-79987)).
- **Monsoon onset:** normal date 1 June ([The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/southwest-monsoon-likely-hit-kerala-june-5-imd-124627)).
- **Festivals:**
  - Attukal Pongala in Feb/Mar ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attukal_Pongala)).
  - Theyyam from mid-Oct to May ([Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/faq/can-i-participate-theyyam-rituals-as-part-of-a-cultural-tour)).
  - Pulikali on the fourth day of Onam ([Kerala Tourism](https://keralatourism.org/onam/onam-games/pulikali)).
  - Aranmula ([Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/champions-boat-league/boat-races/aranmula-boat-race)).
  - Kalpathy in November ([Kerala Tourism](https://keralatourism.org/kerala-article/kalpathy-ratholsavam/684)).
  - Sabarimala from mid-Nov to Makaravilakku on about 14 Jan ([AIR News](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/sabarimala-ayyappa-temple-opens-today-for-mandala-makaravilakku-pilgrimage-season)).
  - Cochin Carnival and Pappanji ([Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/event/cochin-carnival/114/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pappanji)).
- **Spices:**
  - Anjarakandy cinnamon estate, 1767, reputedly Asia's largest ([Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/destination/cinnamon-valley-anjarakandy/501), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anjarakkandy)).
  - Nutmeg grown in Thrissur, Ernakulam and Kottayam; mace comes from the same fruit ([ICAR-IISR](https://www.spices.res.in/crops/nutmeg)).
  - Vasco da Gama at Kappad, May 1498 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasco_da_Gama)).
  - Kudampuli is *Garcinia gummi-gutta* ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malabar_matthi_curry)).
